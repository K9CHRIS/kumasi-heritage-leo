📚 September Service Activity: Heritage Literacy Campaign 2026

Kumasi Virtual Heritage Leo-Lions Club, Kumasi Adehyee Leo Club, and Kumasi Oasis Leo Club present the Heritage Literacy Campaign 2026 — empowering young minds through education, mentorship, and responsible decision-making across Kakokrom (Western Region), Afienya Mataheko (Greater Accra Region), and Anyeduase (Ashanti Region).

"Build young minds today. Strengthen our heritage tomorrow."

Support via MTN MoMo: 059 439 5131 (Kumasi Heritage Leo Lions Club)
Support & Donate 💛 Locations: Kakokrom, Afienya Mataheko, Anyeduase

🔥 Online Session: The Good, The Bad & The Ugly Side of Social Media

What impact does social media have on us? Join Lions International District 418 Ghana (Kumasi Heritage Virtual Leo-Lions Club & Takoradi Nkuranhye Virtual Leo-Lions Club) with facilitator Lion Andre Amenuku-Agble (CEO of VOXDIGITS Communications LLC) to explore the benefits, risks, and realities we often overlook.
