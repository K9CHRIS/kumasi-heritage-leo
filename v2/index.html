Lions Global Causes

Our Core Service Pillars

Addressing vital community needs in Kumasi and rural communities through dedicated Lions initiatives.

Vision Screening & Sight

Organizing community eye health screenings, providing corrective eyeglasses, and preventing avoidable blindness in under-resourced areas.

Diabetes & Health Screenings

Conducting blood sugar tests, hypertension checks, and public health awareness workshops to foster healthier lifestyles.

Hunger Relief & Nutrition

Distributing food packages and nutritious meals to local orphanages, vulnerable families, and community care centers.

Environmental Sustainability

Leading tree planting campaigns, sanitation cleanup drives, and educating schools on environmental conservation.

September Service Activity: Heritage Literacy Campaign 2026

Kumasi Virtual Heritage Leo-Lions Club, Kumasi Adehyee Leo Club, and Kumasi Oasis Leo Club present the Heritage Literacy Campaign 2026 — empowering young minds through education, mentorship, and responsible decision-making across Kakokrom (Western Region), Afienya Mataheko (Greater Accra Region), and Anyeduase (Ashanti Region).

"Build young minds today. Strengthen our heritage tomorrow."
